I build AI agents for companies that have to trust them. Principal AI Engineer at [Zazmic](https://zazmic.ai), a Google Cloud Premier Partner, where I lead and mentor the ML engineers and still write a lot of the code. Multi-agent systems on Google ADK, the Gemini Enterprise Agent Platform and Claude, for clients in media, healthcare, finance and retail supply chains.

Before this: medical claims automation at 50,000 claims a day, code security agents with Qwiet AI, demand forecasting that moved real inventory. The domain is always new. The ability to figure it out is not.

**I write every week at [umairahmad-ua.github.io](https://umairahmad-ua.github.io).**
A Sunday note on what changed in AI that week, and a deep dive most Wednesdays on something I built or broke. Every claim has a dated source.

#### Things I care about

- Agents that survive contact with a finance team: evals in CI, cost per task, human gates that people actually use.
- Tool contracts that do not rot. MCP, typed outputs, permission models for agents that touch infrastructure.
- Taking a model from a two-week proof of concept to something a nurse, a planner or a fraud analyst depends on.
